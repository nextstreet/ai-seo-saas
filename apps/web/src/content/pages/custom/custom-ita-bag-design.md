An effective custom Ita Bag starts with the display collection, not a list of decorative options. Pin size, photocard sleeves, plush accessories, and the way the bag will be carried determine the useful window area, insert construction, and overall bag format.

## Choose the product architecture first

Decide whether the project needs a backpack, tote, crossbody, or compact bag. Then map the display zone and storage zone separately. A larger clear panel can improve visibility, but it also changes structure, privacy, weight distribution, and the space left for everyday storage.

## Build a decision-ready specification

Record target dimensions, intended display objects, exterior and lining materials, window shape, insert attachment, closure, straps, hardware finish, decoration method, packaging, and expected quantity. Treat material thickness, color matching, minimum order quantity, sampling cost, and lead time as supplier-confirmed details because they depend on the final construction and production setup.

## Review the sample as a complete system

Place real display items in the sample. Check visibility, pin security, insert removal, zipper access, strap comfort, seams around the window, surface finish, and whether the loaded bag keeps its shape. Capture revisions in writing before production approval.

## Prepare the inquiry

Send a concise brief with reference images, annotated dimensions, artwork files, target quantity, destination, and required date. That gives a production team enough context to identify open decisions instead of guessing at a quotation.
